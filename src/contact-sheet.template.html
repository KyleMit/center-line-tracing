The engine, against the drawings it has to trace

Every drawing in inputs/ at the shipped default — raster scale 8 with automatic width-aware pruning, and scale 2 for the two drawings that measured better there. Drag any frame to wipe between the original design and the traced result. Median error across the sheet is 0.0188 of source ink; at this size most of the disagreement is thinner than the line it sits on.

All frames

Each frame has its own handle — drag it, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Difference shows only where the two disagree: white is a perfect match.

What you are looking at

Left of the handle: the original input SVG.
Right: the emitted output, extracted at the default raster scale and pruned at the strength the selector chose. Both sides rendered by the same rasterizer, same pixel size, same white ground — so anything you see at the seam is the tracing, not the render.

The two exceptions

sun-square and landscape-square are outlined in amber and run at scale 2. Both are dense scribble fills whose taper tails resolve into extra skeleton structure at a finer raster. sun-square alone is 37% better at scale 2 than at scale 4.
